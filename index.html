Scientific Facial Morphometry & FAQ Guide

What is HSAY (How sexy are you?) and how does it work?

HSAY is a local, rule-based facial morphometry tool for portrait and profile photography. It measures photo-derived proportions, bilateral balance, skin-presentation proxies, and estimated single-image depth proxies. Roll and scale are normalised; a photograph is not converted into clinical 3D data or physically rectified to an 85 mm lens.

How are the three report indices calculated?

HSAY computes three transparent, code-defined indices: (1) Structural Balance (0–100) from proportions, eyes, bilateral balance, skin presentation, configuration, and cross-feature harmony; (2) Feature Definition & Freshness (0–100) from a selected sex-profile fit, lower-face geometry, eye configuration, freshness proxy, symmetry, and skin presentation; and (3) a PSL-inspired geometry heuristic (1.0–9.0). None of these is a prediction of social attractiveness, sexual attractiveness, a community score, or a population percentile.

What are fWHR, Canthal Tilt, and the Eye Geometry Composite?

fWHR (Facial Width-to-Height Ratio): cheekbone width divided by upper-face height (nasion to stomion) in this implementation. Canthal Tilt: the angle between inner and outer eye corners. Eye Geometry Composite: a transparent photo proxy combining tilt, relative lower scleral exposure, palpebral aspect ratio, and orbital compactness; it is not a clinical measurement or social ranking.

Is my photo private and secure?

100% Client-Side Privacy: All computer vision, MediaPipe neural networks, OpenCV image processing, and calculations run strictly inside your local browser via WebAssembly and WebGL. Your photos are never uploaded to any remote server or stored in any database.
